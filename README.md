A single one-line 1-star review, written by a houseplant about the human who owns it.

> One star. He waters me every Sunday whether I'm thirsty or drowning, then tells guests I'm 'low maintenance' while I sit here dying in a pot he bought because it matched the couch.

`main.py` writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, under 280 characters).
